💡 AS-62: Social Value Technical Assessment and EA Review
SUBJECT (App or other)
Thrive Platform
Description
Thrive Social Value is a software platform used to measure, manage, evidence and report social value across an organisation, contracts, suppliers and projects. In simple terms, it helps organisations prove the positive impact they create beyond just delivering a service or product. Social value means the wider economic, social and environmental benefits created through work or procurement, such as local jobs, apprenticeships, volunteering, community investment, carbon reduction, wellbeing improvements, supplier diversity and support for local businesses. In UK public procurement, the Social Value Model is used to select relevant outcomes and criteria for contracts. Thrive helps organisations: 1. Capture social value activities For example, training delivered, volunteering hours, local employment, environmental initiatives or community support. 2. Measure impact It can convert activities into measurable outcomes and, where appropriate, financial proxy values. 3. Collect evidence It supports auditability by storing proof, updates, supplier submissions and supporting documents. 4. Report progress Dashboards and reports help show performance against social value commitments, contract obligations or ESG objectives. 5. Manage suppliers and projects Organisations can track social value delivery across multiple contracts, frameworks, suppliers, departments or programmes. In a procurement or government context, Thrive would typically be used to track whether suppliers are actually delivering the social value promises they made during tendering, rather than leaving those commitments as static bid responses. For GCA, it would support supplier onboarding, reporting, dashboards, evidence capture and performance monitoring against agreed social value measures. Thrive is a social value management and reporting platform that helps organisations turn social impact commitments into measurable, evidenced and reportable outcomes.
